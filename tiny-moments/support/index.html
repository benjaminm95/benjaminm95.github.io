  1. Open the Tiny Moments app and navigate to your baby's profile.
  2. Tap the + button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Choose a milestone category (e.g., First Steps, First Words, First Foods).
  4. Add a date, photo, and any notes you would like to remember.
  5. Tap Save to record the milestone.

  1. Open the Tiny Moments app.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (gear icon) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Upgrade to Pro.
  4. Review the Pro features and pricing, then tap Subscribe or Purchase.
  5. Confirm the purchase with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password.

Pro benefits include: Ad-free experience, unlimited PDF exports, premium milestone templates, and more.

If you have previously purchased Pro, tap Restore Purchases on the upgrade screen to restore your subscription.

  1. Open the milestone you would like to export.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow) in the top-right corner.
  3. Select Export as PDF from the menu.
  4. Choose a layout template for your PDF.
  5. Preview the PDF, then tap Save or Share to send it to Files, email, Messages, or another app.

Free users can export a limited number of PDFs per month. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited PDF exports.

  1. Open the milestone you would like to share.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Choose how you would like to share:
    • Share as Image: Creates a beautifully designed milestone card you can send via Messages, WhatsApp, or social media.
    • Share as PDF: Exports a detailed PDF with photos and notes.
    • Copy Link: If you have created a shareable milestone card.
  4. Select the app or contact you would like to share with.

All sharing is initiated by you -- Tiny Moments never automatically shares your data with anyone.

  1. From the home screen, tap your baby's name at the top to open the profile switcher.
  2. Tap Add Baby to create a new profile.
  3. Enter the baby's name, birth date, and an optional photo.
  4. Tap Save to add the new profile.

You can switch between profiles at any time by tapping the name at the top of the home screen. Each baby has their own separate milestone timeline.

Since all data is stored locally on your device, deleting the Tiny Moments app will permanently remove all your milestone data, baby profiles, and saved photos from the app. This action cannot be undone.

We recommend exporting your important milestones as PDFs or images before deleting the app. If you have a Pro subscription, your subscription status can be restored by re-downloading the app and tapping "Restore Purchases."
