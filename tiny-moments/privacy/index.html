Introduction

Tiny Moments ("the App") is developed by CraftedByDev ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your family. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Our core principle: Your precious family moments belong to you. Tiny Moments is designed with a privacy-first approach -- your personal data stays on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

Information We Collect

Tiny Moments may collect or access the following types of information:

  • Photos: The App accesses your device's photo library only with your explicit permission. Photos you add to milestones are stored locally on your device only.
  • Baby Names & Dates: Any baby names, birth dates, or milestone dates you enter are stored locally on your device.
  • Milestone Data: All milestone entries, notes, and related content are stored locally on your device.

Important: None of the personal data listed above -- photos, names, dates, or milestone information -- is ever transmitted to external servers. All data remains on your device under your control.

Photo Library Access

Tiny Moments requests access to your device's photo library so you can attach photos to your baby's milestones. This access is only granted with your explicit permission through the iOS system permission prompt.

You can revoke photo access at any time through your device's Settings app. If you revoke access, previously added photos that are already stored within the App will remain, but you will not be able to add new photos.

Push Notifications

Tiny Moments may request permission to send push notifications to remind you of upcoming milestones or to provide helpful tips. You can enable or disable notifications at any time through your device's Settings app. We do not collect or store any data related to notification delivery.

In-App Purchases

Tiny Moments offers a Pro upgrade through Apple's StoreKit framework. All purchase transactions are processed and managed entirely by Apple. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, including credit card numbers or Apple ID credentials.

For details about how Apple handles purchase data, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The free tier of Tiny Moments displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information and use advertising identifiers to serve relevant ads. This data is handled by Google in accordance with their privacy practices.

We do not share any of your personal data (photos, names, milestone data) with AdMob or any advertising partner. Upgrading to Tiny Moments Pro removes all advertisements.

For details about how Google handles advertising data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We recognize that Tiny Moments is used to record information about children, including infants and toddlers. We take the privacy of children's data very seriously.

All children's data -- including names, birth dates, milestone information, and photos -- is stored exclusively on the parent's or guardian's device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any children's data on external servers. The App does not have user accounts, and children's data is never shared with third parties.

Parents and guardians maintain full control over all data entered into the App and can delete it at any time by removing the data within the App or by uninstalling the App from their device.

Data Storage & Security

All personal data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS data storage mechanisms. Since your data never leaves your device, its security is tied to your device's own security measures, including passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

We recommend keeping your device software up to date and using a strong passcode to protect your data.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual milestones, photos, or baby profiles at any time within the App. To delete all data associated with Tiny Moments, simply uninstall the App from your device.

Third-Party Services

Tiny Moments integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Apple StoreKit: For processing in-app purchases. Apple's Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements in the free tier. Google's Privacy Policy

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
