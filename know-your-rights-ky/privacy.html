Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

Overview

Know Your Rights KY ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, use, and share — which is none.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No cookies or identifiers
  • No advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • No third-party analytics tools
  • No location data
  • No camera or microphone access

Local Storage

The App stores your Emergency Card information (attorney contact, emergency contact, personal notes) locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or have access to any payment information. All transactions are handled by Apple under their privacy policy.

Offline Operation

The App is designed to work completely offline. All content is bundled within the app. No internet connection is required to use any feature.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this page. Since we collect no data, we don't anticipate changes.
