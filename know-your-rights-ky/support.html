Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock all topics?

The first topic in every section is free. To unlock all 56+ topics, tap any locked topic or the "Unlock" banner and complete the one-time $0.99 purchase. No subscriptions.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you've already purchased and need to restore (new device, reinstall), open the paywall screen and tap "Restore Purchases." This will restore your access at no additional cost.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All content is stored on your device. No internet connection is needed to read any topic or use the Emergency Card.

Is this legal advice?

No. Know Your Rights KY provides general legal information for educational purposes. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. If you are facing a legal situation, consult a licensed Kentucky attorney.

Is my data private?

Yes. The app collects zero data. Your Emergency Card information is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for details.
